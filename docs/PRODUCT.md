# Product

## Register

product

## Users
A sender in the US paying a synthetic recipient in Thailand, shown live on a projector to hackathon judges, and the presenter driving the demo from a laptop. The job: send USD, see exactly what arrives in THB, confirm once, and watch the money move with honest evidence for each leg.

## Product Purpose
CentMe is a test-mode USD to THB remittance demo. Funding, conversion and payout are simulated; settlement is real test USDC on Avalanche Fuji. Success is a judge following Send, Review & pay, Track and Receipt without explanation, and trusting the labels on every step.

## Brand Personality
Plain, trustworthy, honest. Feels like a real bank app, speaks in human money words first ("They receive ฿343.00"), and never overclaims what is simulated.

## Anti-references
Crypto-exchange dashboards (dense tickers, neon, dark trading UIs), wallet-connect flows, fake chain evidence, emoji icons, silent re-pricing, marketing-style hero pages.

## Design Principles
1. One deliberate review-and-confirm moment before money moves; one primary action per screen.
2. Honest evidence labels on every leg (SIMULATED, TESTNET · Fuji, MOCK).
3. Plain language first, technical detail behind a disclosure.
4. Every error says what happened and offers one button to fix it.
5. Motion follows real status changes, never a fixed timer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA: body text at least 16px, contrast at least 4.5:1, visible focus rings, color never the only signal, keyboard-complete, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. English default with a Thai toggle.
